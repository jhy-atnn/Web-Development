Character, village, clan, team, tailed-beast, kekkei-genkai, Akatsuki, jutsu, and nature records are requested from the
live Dattebayo Naruto API. Narrative summaries and explicitly stated eye colours are requested from the Naruto Wiki's
MediaWiki API. The project contains no local JSON source for Naruto facts or descriptions.

Pictures can come from Dattebayo, matching Naruto Wiki pages, or local presentation assets. Clan symbols and Wiki
summaries may be unavailable when the Wiki has no matching page or file. If Dattebayo is unavailable, API-powered
pages and features will not load until the service returns.
